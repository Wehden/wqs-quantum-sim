### G1-05: Source-pinned native work accounting

Select QuTiP's native `vern7` integrator for SE, ME, MC and time-dependent propagators, with server-owned `atol=1e-10`, `rtol=1e-9`, `nsteps=100000` and MC collapse-search `norm_steps=25`. Step size is derived from the resolved coefficient segments; users cannot supply integrator names or native option dictionaries. Static spectra and unique steady-state solves use the selected direct CPU linear-algebra paths, not an ODE integrator. No custom numerical integrator, native source fork or request-provided callback is introduced.

Use a trusted, child-local subclass of `IntegratorVern7` that delegates unchanged native mathematics and records entry to `set_state`, `integrate` and `mcstep`. Register its fixed private name through `SESolver`, `MESolver` and `MCSolver.add_integrator` inside the numerical child. Passing the class directly as `method` hit a native options lookup `KeyError` in discovery; do not rely on that path. The registry and counter are fresh per child/job. Charge before entry, retain charges on exceptions, and never reset the ledger at an output time, segment boundary, trajectory restart or collapse search. `mcstep` includes calls from collapse-location searches. State initialization, including resets, is counted separately.

The pinned Cython source has 10 integration stages, 16 stages including dense output, at most 3 RHS evaluations on initialization, and at most 6 extra RHS evaluations for dense output in a native call. The error loop checks `nsteps > max_step` after computing a trial, so a conservative successful-or-failed call allowance is `10*(nsteps+1)+6`, not `nsteps`. For counts S (set-state entries), A (integrate entries) and M (mcstep entries), publish:

`rhs_evaluations_upper_bound = 3*S + (10*(100000+1)+6)*(A+M)`.

This is a **source-derived upper bound**, not an actual RHS count, elapsed-time prediction or total floating-point-operation estimate. Record S/A/M separately. A source/tableau/options change invalidates the bound and requires updated tests before that backend is enabled. The formula includes native failures; it is intentionally conservative. Count requested/completed trajectories, completed segments and output points separately. An incomplete result is never called successful because its counter is within a bound.

**No new job-wide cumulative work cutoff is introduced.** `nsteps` limits each native advance, and `norm_steps` each collapse search; they do not bound the total number of MC advances or jumps. The cumulative ledger reports work across the entire job without claiming a precomputed universal maximum. Existing dimension, memory, trajectory, coefficient and output admission bounds still apply, as do native convergence failures and existing cancellation. There is no new simulation wall-time cap, CPU-second quota, account/job-count cap or automatic truncation of trajectories. This resolves the earlier tentative ledger proposal without inventing an unmeasured global work ceiling.

For scqubits record eigensystem calls, raw basis dimension, requested levels and operator transforms; reuse one eigensystem. For JoSIM record requested grid-row upper bound, returned rows, trace count, binary identity and native exit status. Output rows do not establish a bound on JoSIM's hidden Newton/adaptive iterations. Scheduler/OS resource consumption remains a distinct observed metric. Do not invent comparable RHS counts for those backends or expose fake fractional solver progress.

### G1-04: Pulse definitions

Adopt `constant`, plain `square`, truncated `gaussian`, `flattop_cosine` and numeric `sampled` envelopes. Recommend deferring `drag` and positive `square.rise` for schema v1, as allowed by the approved design; approval of this packet accepts that deferral. Plain square accepts omitted/zero rise only. `flattop_cosine` explicitly accepts `0 < rise <= (stop-start)/2`: its entrance is `(1-cos(pi*(t-start)/rise))/2`, its exit is the symmetric reversed edge, and its middle is one. Gaussian is `exp(-0.5*((t-center)/sigma)**2)` within its declared support; sigma must be positive. Zero outside support is explicit. Carrier phase is `2*pi*detuning_GHz*t_ns + phase_rad`, using the original model time even across segments.

Segment every discontinuity, including truncated nonzero Gaussian endpoints, with one-sided coefficient values and no duplicated times inside a segment. Preserve native state and the original propagator time reference; there is no physical state reset or cross-job chaining. Plain square edges are exact segments, not interpolated ramps. Linear sampled envelopes are the caller's declared physical function, not a smooth function inferred from the data. Every modulation and support edge is represented independently of output times.

For smooth pieces choose grid spacing from the analytic second-derivative bound `h^2*sup(abs(f''))/8`; use a sum of operator Frobenius-norm bounds and interval lengths to certify the coefficient-induced generator error. Apply 2pi to Hamiltonian amplitudes once; commutator ME bounds include their factor of two. The coefficient approximation budget is 1e-6; it is separate from native solver tolerances and MC sampling uncertainty. MC certification bounds the unnormalized generator approximation, not every normalized conditional trajectory or a noisy sample comparison. Grid doubling is a required diagnostic, not the certificate. Require at most 65,536 combined knots and 16 scalar channels, count conjugates/copies, and refuse unresolved grids without coarsening. Derive max_step from the smallest resolved feature inside each nonempty segment, rather than tiny stepping across long empty intervals. Gaussian/flattop boundary and adversarial convergence tests are mandatory G2 gates before advertisement.

## Exact result envelope proposal

Use uncompressed numeric NPZ, with a scalar Unicode `metadata_json` and no object arrays or pickle. Proposed limits:

- At most 6 MiB of numeric payload, 64 total members including metadata, ASCII member names up to 64 characters and rank at most four.
- Metadata JSON at most 64 KiB UTF-8; count its actual NumPy Unicode storage as `4 * len(metadata_json)`, bounded by 256 KiB.
- Summary JSON at most 64 KiB. Count actual escaped/encoded bytes, not characters.
- Bound each NPY header to 4,096 bytes and reserve 256 bytes of ZIP/name overhead per member plus 1,024 archive bytes. Validate the emitted archive against this formula.

A conservative complete NPZ bound is `B = numeric_bytes + 4*metadata_characters + 4352*member_count + 1024`, at most 6,833,152 bytes at these ceilings. Base64 is exactly `4*ceil(B/3)`, at most 9,110,872 bytes. With the bounded summary and 256 bytes of callback JSON framing, the complete numerical output file is at most 9,176,664 bytes, below the existing 12-MiB file and artifact-field limits and 16-MiB request limit. These totals include metadata and archive overhead; compression is not assumed.

TASK-008 must verify NumPy's actual header/ZIP framing and worst-case Unicode/summary escaping, charge simultaneous serialization copies, and reject any emitted object exceeding its planned bound. If the measured encoder violates the formula, admission remains closed until the formula or encoding is corrected. Existing circuit archives, summaries and canonical bytes remain unchanged.

## G1-06: Admission plan and algorithm certificates

Normalize dimensions and shape products with checked integer arithmetic before allocating NumPy/native arrays. Reject malformed, nonfinite, non-Hermitian and over-budget inputs before enqueueing. An admitted plan contains exact result shapes/dtypes, allocation line items, coefficient plan, selected native algorithm and policy revision. API validation imports only Pydantic/stdlib and bounded NumPy; no SciPy/QuTiP/scqubits/CUDA import is permitted. Worker revalidation must reproduce the plan under the pinned policy before native construction.

Initial structural envelopes are at most eight ordered modes (dimensions >=1), 256 static terms, 32 named matrices, and Lindblad Hilbert dimension <=256, dense ME evolution/steady-state dimension <=32, at most 1,000 serial MC trajectories, at most 32 named observables and at most 16 collapse channels. These are proposed tested-family envelopes; each operation is additionally restricted by its allocation certificate and the 6 MiB result budget. Liouville and propagator shapes are charged at D^4, so these dimensions alone never imply admission. scqubits remains within the measured raw-basis D<=512 / levels<=32 envelope for all four classes. JoSIM uses the tested positive subset below. Larger dimensions require another measured profile, not a hidden override.

Allocation accounting is algorithm-specific. Charge input/constructed operators and tensor/Kronecker temporaries, all coefficient times/values/conjugates/interpolation copies, retained states/observables, solver buffers and simultaneous serialization copies. For pinned interpolated vern7 reserve **24 state-shaped complex128 buffers** (16 derivative stages plus native state/front/previous/temporary/FSAL and boundary-copy slack), in addition to generator/model matrices and retained outputs. This replaces the rejected generic eight-array estimate. ME vectors have D^2 cells; propagator states can be D-by-D or D^2-by-D^2 matrices. Each dense complex cell costs 16 bytes. Sparse inputs are charged for any downstream densification; sparse construction alone does not authorize a dense operation.

For dense eigensystem, unique-state/SVD and scqubits constructor paths, an algorithm certificate records native matrix copies, bounded LAPACK workspace-query outputs and constructor temporaries for the pinned build. Do not assign an invented universal workspace multiplier. The API uses a frozen certificate table shipped with the same policy/image; it does not run LAPACK workspace discovery. Reserve 512 MiB import/native headroom and keep total charged allocation <=2 GiB within the existing CPU 3 GiB AS / 4 GiB container profile. The certificate also covers parent/child handoff and base64/JSON buffer coexistence, not merely the final array. An operation with a missing certificate or failed largest-admitted-shape test stays disabled; G2 populating/proving those certificates is implementation acceptance, not an unresolved policy choice or a waiver.

Density input remains bounded to D256 with trace/Hermiticity/PSD tolerances 1e-10 as above. Hamiltonian Hermiticity uses a scale-aware 1e-10 residual against max(1,Frobenius norm). For a dense static Liouvillian of size L=D^2, set tau=100*eps64*L*max(s_max,tiny64). Require exactly one singular value <=tau and, when L>1, the next singular value >10*tau; otherwise refuse a nonunique/ambiguous stationary state. Require stationary residual ||L*vec(rho)||2 <=10*tau*max(1,||vec(rho)||2) and validate the solved state; do not select or repair an arbitrary state. FluxQubit's 2x2 capacitance matrix must be finite, positive definite and have reciprocal condition >=sqrt(float64 epsilon), with finite inverse and residual check. This is a scale-invariant numerical-stability rule, not an arbitrary GHz cap. Boundary and near-degenerate tests must establish the declared residuals before advertisement.

## G1-12: Result and failure contract

`PhysicsExecutionResult.arrays` is an ordered mapping of stable ASCII member names to non-object NumPy arrays, with canonical float64/complex128 or required integer dtypes and rank <=4. Metadata is finite JSON and is serialized only as a scalar Unicode `metadata_json` member. The serializer owns dtype/shape/byte verification and the complete NPZ/base64/JSON envelope bound above. No native solver object or executable serialization is accepted.

Result members are: Lindblad `times` (T), `expectations` (T,O for evolution; O for steady state), optional `states` (T,D for SE; T,D,D for ME/MC), `eigenvalues` (K), optional `eigenvectors` (D,K), optional `steady_state` (D,D), and `propagators` (T,D,D for SE; T,D^2,D^2 for ME). Propagators include the first requested time identity, always referenced to that original time. MC stores ensemble density matrices, not individual trajectory axes. Operation-inapplicable members are absent. Zero-collapse ME remains a density/superoperator path. MC requires a pure input and at least one nonzero collapse channel; otherwise return 422 before enqueueing and direct the caller to SE/ME. No undocumented native solver fallback or meaningless trajectory count is attached to a deterministic request. The dedicated zero-collapse-MC refusal is a G2 API regression case.

Steady state accepts up to 32 named observables and boolean `return_state` (default true). Evaluate expectations against the same unique validated stationary density and preserve observable order. Omit `expectations` when no observables are supplied; omit `steady_state` only when `return_state=false`. The internal density is always computed, included in allocation accounting and validated, independently of output selection. No time axis is fabricated for stationary expectations. TASK-016 retains its approved named-expectations/optional-state behavior.

The operation/field compatibility matrix is explicit:

| Operation | Accepted solver/model controls | Refused controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| evolve/se | Pure state, times, observables, optional retained kets | Dissipation, trajectories, seed, spectrum/vector controls |
| evolve/me | Pure-to-density or mixed input, times, observables, optional density series; collapse list may be empty | Trajectories, seed, spectrum/vector controls |
| evolve/mc | Pure state, nonzero collapse channel, times, observables, 1..1,000 serial trajectories, seed, optional ensemble densities | Mixed input, empty/effectively-zero collapse list, spectrum/vector controls |
| spectrum | Static Hamiltonian, evals_count and optional eigenvectors | Drives, dissipation, initial_state, times, observables, MC controls |
| steadystate | Static Lindbladian, named observables, return_state and unique-state/residual checks | Drives, initial_state, times, MC/vector controls |
| propagator/se or me | Times and Hamiltonian; ME allows dissipation, including an empty collapse list | Initial_state, observables, MC/vector controls; SE dissipation |

Ignored operation-specific inputs are rejected, rather than silently discarded. Generated structural models express these operation branches, and semantic checks cover effective nonzero channels and physical constraints.

scqubits uses `eigenvalues` (K), optional `eigenvectors` (D,K), named operator matrices (K,K) and an explicit export matrix/basis specification. JoSIM uses `times` (T) and `traces` (T,K), with ordered stable trace names and SI units in metadata. There is no cross-job state. All cumulative native work counters and the conservative RHS upper bound are serialized as decimal strings, so JavaScript cannot silently round large counts. Counts are observations and never progress fractions. Output state validation uses trace/Hermiticity tolerance 1e-7 and PSD floor -1e-8, distinct from the stricter input-state tolerance; violating results fail without repair.

MC defaults to effective seed 42 when omitted, records the effective seed and pinned NumPy/QuTiP SeedSequence mapping, and runs serial native trajectories. Same-image seed repeat is required; bitwise cross-backend reproducibility is not promised.

New 422 errors add an escaped JSON Pointer within the existing envelope and create no job. Bounded worker failures distinguish invalid revalidation/profile, native convergence, memory/resource exhaustion, result-size overflow and nonunique/unstable numerical results without returning raw model text, paths or tracebacks. Known failures are terminal; only existing transport/placement retries apply. Partial native results do not produce success. Cancellation and SSE resume retain existing behavior; no new progress percentage, automatic precision relaxation, trajectory truncation or state repair is introduced.
